# Label printer proxy

An AirPrint queue that finds the label on whatever a phone or laptop prints
and fits it to a label roll. These are the words used for the parts of an
incoming print job.

## Language

**Label**:
The region of a page that should end up on the roll: the part a carrier
expects you to stick on the parcel.
_Avoid_: Crop, box, ticket

**Sheet**:
A page that carries a label together with other material, such as
instructions or a list of drop-off shops.
_Avoid_: Document, form

**Cut line**:
A rule drawn by the carrier marking where the label separates from the rest
of a sheet. Usually dashed, often with a scissors icon.
_Avoid_: Dashed line, separator, divider

**Instruction page**:
A page in a job with no label on it at all, for example a cover page of
"how to return your item" steps.
_Avoid_: Cover page, text page, blank page (a blank page has no ink at all)

**Border**:
A box drawn around a label or a sheet. A border around a sheet encloses the
cut line and the label together.
_Avoid_: Frame, outline
